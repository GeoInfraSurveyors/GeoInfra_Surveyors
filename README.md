# GeoInfra Surveyors

GeoInfra Surveyors specializes in converting survey records, engineering drawings, and infrastructure data into accurate digital formats. We support government departments, infrastructure developers, utility companies, and engineering consultants with GIS, CAD, mapping, and spatial data solutions.

Our expertise includes land records digitization, corridor mapping, cadastral mapping, utility asset mapping, and spatial database creation for roads, railways, pipelines, and urban infrastructure.

Who We Are

About GeoInfra

GeoInfra Surveyors is a professional geospatial and engineering data solutions firm based in India. Founded with a commitment to precision, we bridge the gap between traditional land survey practices and modern digital geospatial technology.

We serve both government bodies and private-sector clients across India, delivering high-accuracy mapping, GIS database creation, cadastral surveys, and CAD drafting for infrastructure projects of all scales.

Our multidisciplinary team combines expertise in surveying, remote sensing, geographic information systems, and civil engineering drawing to provide end-to-end geospatial solutions — from raw data acquisition to final deliverables.

Whether you need digitization of legacy survey records, a complete GIS database for a smart city project, or corridor mapping for a new highway, GeoInfra Surveyors has the tools, talent, and experience to deliver.
